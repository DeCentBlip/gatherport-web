Project overview

Several balances.

Gatherport is an independent wallet utility in development. We are building a simpler way to review selected Ethereum tokens and consolidate eligible assets into USDC in the same wallet.

In development · Interactive demo available

What we are building

A focused workflow for choosing assets, understanding the conversion and reviewing costs before acting.

  • Ethereum as the initial network
  • USDC as the first destination asset
  • User-selected tokens and amounts
  • Clear output, cost and permission review

What you can try now

Explore an example wallet, change amounts and review an illustrative consolidation.

The current demo uses synthetic data. It does not connect a wallet, fetch live quotes or move assets.

Explore the demo

The intended wallet flow

  1. Connect a supported wallet and choose eligible balances.
  2. Review a current quote, the USDC output and separate network costs.
  3. Check required token permissions and confirm through the wallet.
  4. See the result and transaction status.

Live pricing and execution are planned. Availability and costs will depend on the selected assets and market conditions. Separate token approval transactions may be necessary.

Our focus

Clarity before action. Deliberate asset selection. Control that stays with the wallet owner.

The initial scope does not include cross-chain bridging, custody, yield strategies or automated trading. We are concentrating on making one wallet task easier to understand.
